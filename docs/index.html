LESS CLICKING. MORE DOING.

A shortcut with personality.

  1. Drag a file

    Pick a document, photo, or spreadsheet. Keep it right where it belongs.

  2. Hold Shift

    On Mac and Windows, Shift-drag reveals the wheel. On Linux Wayland, open it from the tray first.

  3. Drop on a format

    Choose a format and let the bird take over. A validated copy lands beside your original.

YOUR DESKTOP IS THE DESTINATION

No upload.
No round trip.
Just your files.

Conversion runs on your computer. Your original stays safe, and existing files are never silently overwritten. The bird celebrates after the output has been checked and saved.

A NEW FORM, A FEW CLICKS AWAY

The everyday essentials.

The wheel only shows routes supported by your files and installed engines.

01 / DOC

From draft to done.

Word to PDF with pictures intact through LibreOffice. Extract text and simple HTML. Mac also includes its own Word renderer.

DOCXPDFHTML
02 / IMG

Picture the possibilities.

Convert pictures, shrink a copy, resize, or place an image inside a Word document. JPEG exports get a clean white background.

PNGJPGPDFDOCX
03 / PDF

A little order, please.

Merge, split, reorder, edit metadata, and extract selectable text. Compression saves a new copy only if it gets smaller.

MERGESPLITMETADATA
04 / DATA

Keep the zeros. Change the format.

Move between CSV, TSV, and XLSX. Preserve identifiers and leading zeros. Export a readable PDF when your table needs to travel.

CSVTSVXLSXPDF
05 / MORE

Bring your own engines.

Install LibreOffice for Word layouts, or Pandoc for document routes. Native Mac adds HEIC, editable PDF reconstruction, and cached TeX compilation.
